Word for word

The lyric is the verse. All of it.

Not a paraphrase, not a retelling, not two lines lifted for a hook. Press play and read along — the words beside are the words you hear, in the order you hear them.

Every song in the app is built this way. The Music Studio takes a chapter, sets it to a style, and leaves the text alone — nothing is trimmed to fit a bar, nothing is reworded to make a rhyme land. The melody has to move around the scripture, not the other way round.

60 songs ship in the app across twelve styles, and any chapter you hand the Studio comes back the same way: your translation, your chapter, word for word.

The highlight paces this passage evenly across the track — a reading guide, not a syllable-accurate transcript. The words and their order are exact.

Questions

The honest version.

Is the music really scripture?
Yes. Every lyric is the text of the chapter. Nothing is paraphrased to make it rhyme and nothing is added. You pick the translation; the song sings it.
What do I get without paying anything?
The whole Bible — all 66 books, every chapter — in the King James Version, read by David. Three song credits to start, three voice questions, and a 30-minute sleep timer.
How does making a song work?
Pick any chapter, pick any of the twelve styles, tap generate. It takes about a minute. A song costs 2 credits and can run up to five minutes. No style is locked behind a plan.
What exactly is a credit?
Credits are only spent generating songs. Packs are 10 for $1.99, 75 for $14.99, or 100 for $19.99, and a Seeker subscription adds 5 credits every month. Nothing else in the app costs credits.
What is Seeker?
$4.99 a month or $34.99 a year. It opens all eight narrator voices, all five translations, unlimited voice questions, the 60-minute sleep timer and the AI visual experience, and it adds 5 credits a month. Monthly comes with a 7-day free trial, annual with a 3-day trial.
Do I need an account to listen on this page?
No. Every song here streams in your browser. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to install.
